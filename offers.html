Reading a voucher

What each part of an offer means

  1. Store & category — who you’re buying from.
  2. Offer headline — what the cashback applies to.
  3. Tracks — how soon the purchase usually appears as Tracked.
  4. Confirms — how long the store usually takes to approve it.
  5. Eligibility & conditions — tap to open the full rules and exclusions.
  6. Rate — the cashback percentage or flat amount, and any cap.
  7. Shop — opens the store through your tracked VOUCHERA link.
