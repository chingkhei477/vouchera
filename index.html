How VOUCHERA works

Four simple steps, no guesswork

Cashback comes from the commission a store pays when you shop through a VOUCHERA link. We share part of it with you once the store confirms your order.

  1. STEP 01

    Discover

    Browse offers by category. Each voucher shows the rate, tracking time and conditions.

  2. STEP 02

    Shop

    Tap Shop on the voucher and complete your order on the store’s own site.

  3. STEP 03

    Track

    Your reward moves from Tracked to Pending to Confirmed as the store reviews the order.

  4. STEP 04

    Withdraw

    Once your confirmed balance reaches ₹100, request a payout to your UPI ID.

Good to know

Quick answers

Does VOUCHERA cost anything?

No. VOUCHERA is free to use. Stores pay us a commission when you shop through our links, and we share part of it with you as cashback.

Will I pay more at the store by using VOUCHERA?

No. You pay the store’s normal price. The cashback comes from the store’s commission, not from your pocket.

Why does cashback take time to confirm?

Stores wait until their return and cancellation window ends before approving commission. Once they confirm, your cashback becomes withdrawable.

How do I withdraw?

When your confirmed cashback reaches ₹100, send a withdrawal request with your UPI ID from the contact page.

My cashback didn’t track. What now?

Send a missing cashback claim with the store name, order ID and order date. Our Cashback Policy explains what we need.

Questions before you shop?

Write to us at support@vouchera.in and we’ll help you out.
