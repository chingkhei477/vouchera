About VOUCHERA

Cashback that reads like a clear receipt

VOUCHERA is an Indian cashback and rewards website based in Manipur. We built it on a simple idea: you should know exactly what you’ll earn — and what could stop it — before you spend a rupee.

Why the voucher?

A promise you can hold in your hand

A paper voucher has always been an honest little thing: a value, a few printed conditions, and a stub you tear off when it’s used. That’s the spirit behind every offer on VOUCHERA.

Each offer card shows its rate, who is eligible, when it tracks, when it confirms, and what cancels it. Nothing hidden behind a click you didn’t expect.

How we earn

Plain and simple

Partner stores pay VOUCHERA a commission when someone buys through our tracked links. We keep a portion to run the service and pass the rest to you as cashback.

You never pay a joining fee, subscription or payout fee.

What we stand for

Four principles, stamped on everything

Clarity

Every rate, timeline and condition is written in plain language before you shop.

Fairness

The same published payout rules for every member: ₹100 minimum, UPI payouts, no fees.

Privacy

We collect only what’s needed to track and pay your cashback, and we never sell your data.

Respect

No pressure tactics or exaggerated claims — just real offers you can choose to use.
